03 / FBI · IC3

FBI/IC3 complaint summary

The saved submissions describe unauthorised NFT sales and transfers, cryptocurrency withdrawals and a social-account takeover. The supplemental complaint lists 109 NFTs on Ethereum and Robinhood Chain; this page focuses on the two Suited Apes above.

Later buyers, recipients and current holders are not accused of involvement merely because they received or hold an NFT.

How IC3 complaints are handled

Personal contact details, addresses, signatures, submission and tracking numbers, and private correspondence are excluded from this public summary. Original records are retained privately.
